Replaro is a mobile app for workout tracking and training programs. This policy explains what data we process, why, where, for how long, and what your rights are.

1. Data we collect

Data Source Purpose Required
Email address, password (hashed by Firebase only) Sign-up Account, sign-in, password reset Yes
Display name Profile Greeting in the app No
Training preferences (goal, level, environment, weekly frequency, unit system) Onboarding / Profile Program recommendation, display Yes (goal/level/environment/frequency)
Workout templates, sessions, sets (reps, weight, duration, distance, RIR/RPE, notes) Use Tracking, progression suggestions, records By use
XP, level, streak, badges, credit ledger Derived on the server Gamification By use
Weight records, body measurements, progress photos, target weight, height Progress tab Progress tracking and comparison No — explicit consent only
Nutrition logs, water intake, personal food library Progress tab Nutrition/hydration tracking No
Excluded exercises, favorites Use Personalization No
Premium status, purchase information RevenueCat / store Access to premium content If you purchase
Language and theme preference Device Stored on device only No

We do not collect: location, contacts, advertising ID, device-ID tracking, third-party analytics, crash reports. The app does not track you across apps or websites.

2. Health data and explicit consent

Weight, body measurements and progress photos may qualify as special-category (health) data (GDPR Art. 9; Turkish KVKK Art. 6). They are processed only if you give a separate explicit consent in the app. Without it the Progress tab stays off and workout tracking is unaffected. You can withdraw consent anytime under Profile → Privacy & data; screens close, existing data is not deleted (export it or delete your account if you wish).

Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling; no automated body analysis is run on photos.

3. Where data is stored; international transfer

Data is stored on Google LLC's Firebase infrastructure (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions). The Firestore location is europe-west1 (Belgium, EU). Your data is therefore processed on servers that may be outside your country. Legal basis for transfer: your explicit consent (in-app statement) and Google's standard contractual clauses / adequacy decisions. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

4. Third parties

Party Role Data shared
Google Firebase (Google LLC) Hosting, auth, database, file storage, server functions All data in Section 1
RevenueCat, Inc. Subscription/purchase management Anonymous user id, purchase status
Google Play / Apple App Store Payment processing Payment details (never reach us)

We do not sell your data or share it with ad networks.

5. Retention

Data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, the profile, all sub-records (workouts, progress, photos, gamification) and the authentication record are permanently deleted on the server; backups are purged within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR Art. 15–22 (and KVKK Art. 11): access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. In the app: - Export my data (Profile → Privacy & data): JSON copy of all your data - Delete my account: irreversible full deletion - Withdraw consent: stop health-data processing

Other requests: destek@replaro.app. We answer within 30 days. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Türkiye: KVKK Board).

7. Children

Replaro is not intended for anyone under 16; we do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. Such accounts are deleted when identified.

8. Security

Access rules are enforced on the server (only the account owner can read their data), XP/credit/membership values cannot be modified by the client, transport is TLS-encrypted, passwords are stored hashed.

9. Medical notice

Replaro is not a medical device; it does not diagnose, treat, or replace medical advice. Consult your doctor before starting an exercise program.

10. Changes

Changes are announced in the app and the "last updated" date is revised; material changes require renewed acceptance.
